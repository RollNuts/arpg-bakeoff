# Market Fit Notes

Status: research input for solo commercial ARPG production.

## Reference Set

Use these games as market and quality references only. Do not copy their
characters, UI, logos, costumes, palettes, silhouettes, lore, weapons, bosses,
or named systems.

| Game | Why It Matters | Absorb | Do Not Copy |

## Recommended Position

Small commercial target:

- solo third-person 3D fantasy action RPG
- 4-8 hour finished game
- five dense handcrafted areas
- five bosses
- one fixed swordfighter protagonist
- simple progression and four weapon families
- Unreal-first vertical slice
- premium indie positioning, not endless live-service scope

Active product definition:

> A lone swordfighter explores the ruins of an ancient sealed kingdom, opens
> shortcuts, strengthens simple gear, and defeats five readable guardians
> through weighty melee combat, careful dodging, limited healing, and altar
> checkpoints.

See `docs/product/game-definition-solo-fantasy-arpg.md`.

## First Store-Facing Promise

The first screenshot/video must show:

- a readable solo swordfighter, not a mannequin
- third-person camera with clear hero/enemy scale
- ancient stone temple identity
- layered lighting, fog, cloth, columns, chains, bells, or altar props
- one enemy telegraph that can be read without text
- one hit with visible impact, flinch, VFX, and audio intent
- HP/stamina/heal UI in the final visual language
- one boss or guardian landmark that sells the game beyond a normal fight
- no graybox, default floor, default sky, debug UI, or unapproved asset pileup
