# AudioBible: 色喰いの王冠

Status: first vertical-slice audio direction.

## Music Identity

Do not use generic orchestral fantasy. The sound should feel like a poisoned
court:

- distorted waltz
- harpsichord
- string quartet
- low choir
- broken music box
- glass tones
- metal impacts
- deep percussion
