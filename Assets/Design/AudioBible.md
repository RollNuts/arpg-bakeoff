# AudioBible: 夜番の砦

Status: active audio direction.

## Music Identity

Dark, tense, and adventurous. Avoid slow ambient-only music. The player should
feel that they are going out at night to hunt monsters, not walking through a
static horror scene.

Useful instruments:

- strings
- low drums
- flute
- horn
- metal percussion
- fire beds
- fort warning horns
